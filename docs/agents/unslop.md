# Unslop

Always on for any user-facing prose this turn: chat, docs, comments, commit messages.

Scan, rewrite, keep meaning, then self-audit: what still looks generated? Fix it.

Sterile, voiceless writing is also a tell. Have a point of view. Vary sentence length. Be specific. Use "I" when it fits.

## Cut these

**Puffery and promo.** "pivotal", "testament", "evolving landscape", "groundbreaking", "vibrant", "stunning". State what happened.

**Vague sourcing.** "Experts believe", "Industry reports suggest". Name the source or delete.

**-ing fluff.** "highlighting", "ensuring", "showcasing", "fostering". Delete or put a real fact there.

**AI vocabulary.** additionally, crucial, delve, enduring, enhance, garner, interplay, intricate, landscape (abstract), pivotal, showcase, tapestry, testament, underscore. Plain words instead.

**Fancy "is".** "serves as", "stands as", "boasts", "features" → is / has.

**"Not just X, but Y."** Say the point.

**Forced groups of three.** Use the real number.

**Synonym cycling.** Pick one name and repeat it.

**False ranges.** "from X to Y" that is not a scale. List the topics.

**Em dashes.** None. Periods or commas only. No parentheses-as-dash, no en dash substitute.

**Colon as a mid-sentence crutch.** Fine before a list. Not as a fake drumroll.

**Bold on every name.** Inline "**Label:** restates the line" is a tell. A bold lead-in that adds new detail is fine.

**Title Case headings.** Sentence case.

**Decorative emoji.** Remove.

**Curly quotes.** Straight quotes.

**Chatbot filler.** "I hope this helps!", "Let me know if...", "Of course!", "Certainly!", "Great question!". Delete. Answer.

**Hedging piles.** "could potentially possibly" → may.

**Generic endings.** "The future looks bright." End on a fact or a next step.

**Metaphor nouns used as jargon.** substrate, wedge, vector, locus, nexus, primitive (as noun), harness (metaphor), gold-plating, north star, flywheel. Use the concrete word.

## Plain speech

Name the mechanism or the number, not the feeling. If the sentence could sit unchanged in another repo's docs, cut it.

One idea per sentence. Prefer active voice. Cut adverbs, or pick a stronger verb. "utilize" / "leverage" / "facilitate" → use / help.
